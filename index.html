What We Do

Harbor Site Watch checks small-business websites for the security and configuration problems that are easy to miss and expensive to ignore — outdated software, missing email protections such as DMARC, and common misconfigurations that leave a site open to attack or make business email look like spam. Every audit ends with a written report that ranks each fix by urgency, in plain language, so you know exactly what matters and what can wait.
